THE YAAB STORY

Built Around Flavour.
Inspired By Heritage.

From childhood memories of berbere and lime to serving Bristol's boldest fried chicken burgers.

Where It Started

Our passion for flavour runs deep. Long before Yaab Burgers was ever an idea, berbere was already a staple in our kitchen. Some of our earliest memories are of mixing it with lime to create a bold, punchy paste that left a lasting impression.

Over time that curiosity became an obsession with flavour. We travelled, explored different cuisines and learned how cultures around the world use spice, heat and balance.

From Side Project To Yaab

What started as brothers experimenting in the kitchen slowly became something bigger. Those early burger sessions were about more than food. They were about pushing flavours, testing ideas and creating something memorable.

That journey led to the berbere burger and eventually Yaab Burgers — a modern take on the flavours that inspired us.

What Does Yaab Mean?

The name Yaab captures that moment of surprise when something tastes better than you expected. That feeling of being caught off guard in the best possible way is exactly what we aim to deliver with every burger.

Today

Today Yaab Burgers is built around one simple idea: serve food packed with flavour and made with care.

Every burger, every box of fried chicken and every order is another chance to create that unforgettable first bite.
